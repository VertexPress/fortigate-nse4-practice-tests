# Errata, FortiGate NSE 4 Practice Tests

This is the single canonical list of known corrections to *FortiGate NSE 4 Practice Tests* (Exam Prep series, Vertex Press), first edition, 2026, written to FortiOS 7.6 and the Fortinet NSE 4 - FortiOS 7.6 Administrator exam (NSE4_FGT_AD-7.6).

Corrections are listed newest first. Each entry names the exam and question, what was wrong, and what is correct. A correction to a keyed answer is the most urgent kind and is published as soon as it is confirmed.

## How to report a suspected error

Open an issue in this repository with the exam number, the question number, and what you believe is wrong. Include the FortiOS 7.6 behaviour or the source that supports the correction if you have it. Reports about a keyed answer are triaged first.

## Corrections

No corrections have been published for this edition yet. Confirmed corrections will be added here.
